Delete my data

Astral Shapes, by Fission Games Studio, United Kingdom.

In the game

  1. Open Settings, then Account.
  2. Tap Delete my data, then DELETE MY DATA.
  3. After 10 seconds, tap YES, DELETE EVERYTHING.

This deletes the game on your phone, your Play Games cloud save if you have one, and everything our server holds about you. If you are offline, the game asks the server again each time it starts.

By email

If you can't open the game, email fission.studio1@gmail.com with your Support ID. It is under Settings, Account, Delete my data, and looks like 3FA9-C1D2. We need it to find your record.

Lost it? If you linked Play Games, reinstall the game, link the same account and delete from there.

We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What stays

Google keeps its own record of what you bought. Diamonds you bought can't come back. Passes and AD-FREE return with Restore purchases.

If an account was banned or suspended, we keep a one-way code of its Play Games ID while the ban lasts, so deleting can't undo the ban. Server backups clear within 30 days.
