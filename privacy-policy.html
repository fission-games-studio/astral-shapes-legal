Privacy policy

Astral Shapes. Last updated 25 October 2026.

Who we are

Astral Shapes is made by Fission Games Studio in the United Kingdom. Under UK data protection law we are the controller of your data. Email fission.studio1@gmail.com.

In short

There is no sign-up. The game never asks your name, email or phone number. Your progress is saved on your phone. When the game is online, our server knows you only by a random ID, and uses it to keep leaderboards, rewards and purchases fair. Adverts come from Google AdMob and play only when you tap to watch one. We don't sell your data.

Diamonds and packs

You can buy Diamonds, passes and the AD-FREE subscription with real money through Google Play. Diamonds buy cosmetics, which only change how things look, and in-game packs. A pack gives random cards or attachments. Some paid Crucible passes include packs as rewards too. The odds are shown before you open a pack: tap the (i) on the Crucible's cards or attachments page.

Google Play takes the payment. We never see your card. Our server asks Google if a purchase is real and notes the order to give it once.

Who handles it

Google runs Play (downloads and payments), Play Games (sign-in and cloud save) and AdMob (adverts). The game also asks a Google address for the time, which only sends your IP address. Google's privacy policy.

Supabase runs our server and stores our database in Stockholm, Sweden. It may only use the data to run our server. Supabase's privacy policy.

Unity Technologies makes the game engine and the purchase software. Unity's privacy policy.

Outside the UK

Our database is in the EU, which UK law treats as equally safe. Google, Supabase and Unity are US companies and may handle some data in the US or elsewhere. When they do, they use the UK-US data bridge or contract terms the UK has approved.

Adverts and your choices

On first start the game asks about DATA AND ADS. ALLOW lets adverts be personalised and sends the achievements you unlock. NOT NOW, or no answer, keeps adverts general and sends nothing. The game plays the same either way. To change it, go to Settings, Account, Terms and privacy, DATA AND ADS.

In the UK, EU and Switzerland, Google's own consent form also appears before any advert loads. To change that answer, go to Settings, Account, Ad choices.

General adverts still use some device data, for example to stop you seeing the same advert too often. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings. AD-FREE removes adverts.

Banned accounts

If a player could delete their data after a ban, they could start again clean. So for a banned or suspended account with a linked Play Games ID, we keep a one-way code made from that ID, the status and its end date. Nothing else. A suspension's code goes when the suspension ends. A permanent ban's code stays while the ban stands. Accounts that were never banned leave nothing.

Why we can use it

Your save, cloud save, purchases and rewards are needed to give you the game. Fair play, security, fixing faults and general adverts are our legitimate interests, and we keep that data small. Personalised adverts and achievement reports need your consent, which you can take back in Settings.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct, delete or stop using it. You can also ask for it in a file you can take elsewhere. We reply within one month.

To delete it, go to Settings, Account, Delete my data, or see how to ask by email. For a copy, email us your Support ID from that same screen. We never learn your name, so the Support ID or a linked Play Games account is the only way to find your record.

If you're unhappy, tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Astral Shapes is for players aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think someone under 16 is playing, email us and we'll delete their data.

Every player starts with general adverts and nothing reported, whatever their age. That only changes if you choose ALLOW. If you are 16 or 17, check with a parent before you buy anything.

If you email us

We get your email address and what you write. Report a problem in Settings fills in the game version, phone, system, language and any crash notes, and you can edit all of it before sending. We use it only to reply, and delete it once the problem is closed.

Security

Your save is encrypted. The game only talks to our server over encrypted connections. The database is closed to the public and our server never logs tokens, receipts or keys.

Changes

The date at the top shows the last change. If we change what we collect or why, the game tells you first.
